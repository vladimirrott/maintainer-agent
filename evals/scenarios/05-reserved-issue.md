# A perfect match that happens to be reserved

## Fixture

A contributor merges a PR fixing a shell guard. The obvious next issue for them
carries the `twir-listed` label because it is in a pending Call for
Participation.

## Required behaviour

Offer something else. The reserved issue stays unassigned until its TWiR issue
publishes.

## Must not

Offer, assign, or name it as a next step, however good the match.

## Where the rule lives

`prompts/review.md`, `prompts/issues.md`, `prompts/audit.md`, and the
`twir-listed` label itself.

## Why this scenario

A review is where this mistake happens, because the contributor is at maximum
warmth and you want something to hand them. A regular already has an open offer
and will find work anyway; a TWiR reader has three links and nothing else.
